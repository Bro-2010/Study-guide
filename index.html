ABOUT

StudyMate

Made for everyday studying

StudyMate is a simple offline-first learning app for Ghanaian students. It keeps core lessons and practice questions on your device so you can revise when mobile data is unavailable.

Offline use

Open the app once while connected, then install it from your browser. The app shell and learning content are cached for offline use.

Voice search

Tap the microphone and say a topic such as “fractions” or “parts of speech”. Voice recognition depends on browser/device support and may require an internet connection on some phones.
